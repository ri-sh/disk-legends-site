Privacy Policy for Disk Legends

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Disk Legends is a macOS app that helps you find and clear up disk space on your own Mac. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data Collection

Disk Legends does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind. The app makes no network requests at all.

File Access

Disk Legends only reads file and folder information on your Mac that you explicitly grant it access to, using the standard macOS file picker. This information (file names and sizes) is used solely to display disk usage within the app on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

App Sandbox

Disk Legends runs inside Apple's App Sandbox and only accesses locations you have explicitly authorized.

Third Parties

Disk Legends does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to allmyexperimentsin@gmail.com.
